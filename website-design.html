A website is often the first decision a client makes about you.

Each site is made for the business behind it. No templates and no filler: real content, considered photography, fast pages and an obvious next step for the visitor, whether that is booking a table, requesting an appointment or starting a conversation.

Because Zenith is also an advisory practice, the website starts from the commercial question: what it needs to achieve, and for whom.

How a website project moves.

Four clear stages, with your approval at each step.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Your business, audience, competitors and goals. We agree what the website must achieve.

  2. 02

    Direction

    Structure, content plan and a visual direction for you to review before the build.

  3. 03

    Design & build

    The full site, shared as a private preview so you can see and comment on the real thing.

  4. 04

    Launch & support

    Final checks on phone and desktop, launch on your domain, then ongoing care.
