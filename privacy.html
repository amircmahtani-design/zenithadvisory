Privacy notice.

How Zenith Advisory Services handles the personal information shared in the course of our work.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Zenith Advisory Services takes the privacy of clients, prospective clients and other contacts seriously. This notice describes, in general terms, how we collect, use and safeguard personal information.

Information we collect

We collect information that is shared with us directly: through the enquiry form on this website, by email, or in conversation. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company and any details you choose to provide about your situation or project.

The enquiry form also records the page it was sent from and the time it was sent. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website.

How we use it

Information is used to respond to enquiries, deliver advisory and website services, communicate with clients and contacts, prepare and keep business records such as proposals, invoices and receipts, and meet legal or regulatory obligations. It is not used for marketing without consent, and is never sold.

Confidentiality

Beyond standard data protection obligations, we treat client information as professionally confidential. Sensitive matters, the existence of engagements and the details of advice are not discussed externally.

Service providers

This website is hosted by Netlify, and enquiries submitted through it are stored in a private, access-controlled system on that platform. Web fonts are delivered by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. We share information with other parties only where strictly necessary for an engagement or required by law.

Storage and security

Personal information is stored securely and retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law, including record-keeping obligations for invoices and tax.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law where it applies, you may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Write to enquiries@zenithadvisory.ae.

Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated from time to time. The version published on this page is the current one.
