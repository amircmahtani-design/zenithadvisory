What to expect from a first conversation.

Is there an obligation?

No. An initial conversation is exploratory. It exists for both sides to understand whether an engagement makes sense, what the scope might look like, and whether the fit is right. There is no commitment until a formal engagement letter or proposal is agreed.

What information do you need upfront?

Very little. A brief description of the situation or the website you have in mind is enough to begin. Sensitive details are best discussed once a confidentiality framework is in place.

How do engagements typically begin?

Most begin with a conversation, followed by a short written summary of what we have understood, what we believe would be useful, and how we would propose to work together.

What about confidentiality?

Confidentiality is fundamental. Information shared in any conversation, formal or informal, is treated as private. Written confidentiality arrangements can be put in place before substantive discussion.
